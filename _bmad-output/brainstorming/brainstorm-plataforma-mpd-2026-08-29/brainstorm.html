Job to Be Done

25 entries · 15 student / 9 coach / 1 log · the longest run of the session

The technique asks what the user is hiring the product to do. The answer arrived in one line and never changed: the encargado de cumplimiento is not hiring software to store documents.

  • ✦ studentCore job: the encargado de cumplimiento makes the MPD effective — not merely documented.
  • ✦ studentThe external auditor is the adversary across the table; asks for documents proving everything is in order.
  • coachThen the product's real output is a defensible evidence trail, not a dashboard: it can answer, months later, “was this control operating, who was responsible, who checked?”
  • ✦ studentControles is the core domain object — many controles auditing different parts of the business, each bound to a specific delito. Soborno y cohecho has its own detection controles.
  • coachOne control engine, then. Controles defined as data and configuration — adding a new delito becomes configuration, not new code. That is what makes a semester scope tractable.
  • ✦ studentSoftware analyzes a business activity — a compra — and emits a score from 0 to 1. Thresholds bucket it into probabilidad baja / media / alta.
  • coachExplainability is the evidence. The auditor asks “why 0.87?”, so every alert must show which señales fired. Rules-based scoring is more defensible than opaque ML — which turns the proposal's named weakness, “no real data for ML”, into a design strength.

Thresholds are pre-defined and configured, the student decided — not tuned ad hoc by the encargado de cumplimiento. That decision has a consequence the coach caught two techniques later: whoever can see the threshold can hide under it.

Figure 3 · the split the student's own list revealed

The student proposed four señales. All four were about who the counterparty is. Naming that pattern produced a second family nobody had listed — and, with it, the scope line that survives all the way to the MoSCoW.

Relacional — who the counterparty is

  • Proveedor recén constituido (days old)
  • Proveedor linked to a family member of the person making the compra
  • A PEP (Persona Expuesta Políticamente) is involved
  • Proveedor has pending lawsuits or judicial record
All four by the student. Needs external data: Poder Judicial, SII. Carries integration risk. — The family-member señal forces a people-and-relationship registry into the data model: a declaración de intereses module, itself a real MPD component.

Intrínseco — the shape of the transaction

  • Fraccionamiento: a compra split into pieces just under the approval threshold
  • The solicitante approves their own compra
  • Price far off market
  • Anomalous timing: weekend, cierre de ejercicio
  • Proveedor shares a bank account, address or phone with an employee
All by the coach. Zero external integrations — semester-safe. This is the natural scope split, and it is the reason the CSV-only decision costs almost nothing.

The decisions the questions forced

  • ✦ studentDesign axiom: a human always makes the decision — computers cannot be held accountable. Matches the proposal's own boundary: the system is not a mechanism of legal determination.
  • ✦ studentThe encargado's actions on an alert: check, review, dismiss, escalate.
  • ✦ studentEvasion: pay in cash — leaves no trace in the ERP at all. Or keep the compra under the threshold so no control fires.
  • coachThresholds create a detection shadow: anyone who knows the threshold hides under it. So threshold configuration must be restricted — who can see it becomes an access-control requirement.
  • coachCounter-señal to under-threshold evasion: agregación temporal — same proveedor, many small compras, the sum crossing the limit over a rolling window.

Figure 4 · two sensors, one blind spot

The student's own evasion — “I could pay in cash” — is what made the canal de denuncias structural rather than a second module bolted on. Controles read data. Cash, favours and a rigged bidding process leave no data. People see those.

Conduct that leaves a data trace — compras, pagos, proveedores, approvals
Conduct that leaves none — efectivo, favores, licitación arreglada

Sensor de datos — los controles

Sees everything in the ERP or the CSV. Scores it, explains it, logs it. Completely blind to the right-hand column.

Sensor de personas — el canal de denuncias

Covers exactly the blind spot. Not an unrelated module: the second half of one instrument.

Figure 5 · the denunciante's three fears are three requirements

The student asked three questions in the voice of the person filing a denuncia. They map one to one onto what the system has to provide.

“Is this fully anonymous?” → Technical guarantees, not a promise: no account, no IP, no metadata leak.
“Who reads this?” → A named recipient chain, plus recusación routing for when the recipient is the accused.
“Does anything actually happen?” → A feedback loop back to an anonymous person — a código de seguimiento: ticket code plus passphrase, so the denunciante can return, see status and answer follow-ups without ever revealing identity.

Trust is a functional requirement, not UX polish. If the denunciante does not trust the channel, the people sensor emits zero data and the MPD is a modelo de papel no matter how good the controles are. — This is the argument that put denuncias in scope. Convergence cut it anyway; see the cut line.

Assumption Reversal

4 entries · 1 student / 3 coach · the shortest technique in the file

Three assumptions, turned over. Each reversal produced a feature that the un-reversed assumption made invisible.

AssumedThe company wants to find wrongdoing.

If it does not, the system must make non-action visible.

Dismissal rates. Aging alerts. Controles never reviewed. Who ignored what. This is how you measure implementación efectiva against modelo de papel — the exact distinction the whole product exists to serve.

AssumedMore alerts means more safety.

Fewer is better.

Alert fatigue trains the encargado de cumplimiento to dismiss everything. Precision over recall — and the system should self-monitor, flagging controles that fire too often.

AssumedDetection is the point.

Prevention is.

The highest-value feature becomes a pre-transaction check that warns the approver before the compra is authorized. Control preventivo against control detectivo is a real compliance distinction, and a very defensible one in the APT.

Noted in the log, honestly The student accepted the assumption list without picking one or adding to it, and answers were going short — signs of fatigue. All three flips are the coach's. It is the only stretch of the session where that is true, and the batch ends one technique later.

Survivor · decided by the student

El motor de controles

Choosing the motor does not delete everything — it absorbs the minimum viable core of several others. Scope discovered by deletion:

  • control definition — which implies the delito mapping
  • the señal library
  • the event store
  • the alert, with its explanation
  • the human decision, with its reason

The ambition, and what it paid for

  • coachThe “unbelievably good” axis for the motor: an encargado de cumplimiento can author a new control without a developer — config-driven, no code. Otherwise it is just a script with a UI.
  • ✦ studentAmbition accepted. Pursue config-driven control authoring, no developer needed — “we have time”.
  • ✦ studentThe auditor must get clear information about how the system works and what the process is — transparency of mechanism, not just of results.
  • coachPayoff: config-driven controles make self-documentation almost free. The same control definition renders twice — as executable logic and as an auditor-readable ficha del control: what it watches, which señales, which weights, which thresholds, and the full history of alerts and human decisions. Choosing no-code authoring buys the auditor-transparency requirement for nearly nothing.

The sources, and the shape they share

  • coachMore Chilean sources: ChileCompra / Mercado Público, Registro de Empresas y Sociedades, Dirección del Trabajo (multas), TDLC (libre competencia), Boletín Comercial, OFAC and UN sanction lists.
  • coachAll of them share one shape: give an identifier, get facts about the entity. So it is not N integrations but one enriquecedor de entidades interface with N adapters — the same one-engine-many-configs pattern as the controles. Stub the adapters, implement one or two for real.
  • coachThe RUT is the universal join key across every Chilean source — the entity-resolution key of the data model.
  • coachEvidence requires point-in-time enrichment: store what the source said when it was checked, with a timestamp. The auditor asks what you knew on 14 March, not what the source says today.

Figure 7 · the loop closing on itself

  • ✦ studentOverride flow: the alert is reviewed by the encargado and closed with a reason; everything gets logged.
  • coachThat cierre con justificación record is the highest-value evidence in the system. Alerts prove the model was watching; overrides prove what the organization decided despite a warning. That is where liability actually lives.
  • coachAnd overrides are themselves a data stream. One gerente overriding forty times is a señal. The engine can run a control over its own usage — the concrete mechanism for the “make non-action visible” flip from Assumption Reversal. Loop closed.
  • coachIndependence, still open: can the encargado close an alert about the person who signs her paycheck? Implies an escalation path to directorio or a comité de ética.

Failure Analysis

11 entries · 2 student / 9 coach · a post-mortem written in advance

Assume the project failed. Say why. The student named the failure first — “bad execution” — and the log records the pushback: too vague, name the mechanism. These are the mechanisms.

F·1

Death by breadthstudent's own pick as biggest risk

Started denuncias, dashboard and ERP integration. Finished none. The most common capstone death.

F·2

Eight weeks of legal research; coding starts week ten.

The derivation chain is the contribution — and also the sinkhole, if it is not timeboxed.

F·3

Blocked on someone else's API.

External data — SII, Poder Judicial, CMF — needed credentials, scraping or legal access that never materialized. Weeks lost to a dependency outside the project.

F·4

No-code control authoring half-built.

Neither works, nor was replaced by hardcoded controles in time. The ambition with no fallback seam.

F·5

Circular evaluationsharpest risk in the file

If the same person designs both the planted casos and the señales that catch them, the evaluation proves nothing. The panelist asks: “you built the data to match your rules.” This is the direct counter-risk to the synthetic-data-as-methodology reframe, and it has to be answered in the thesis rather than avoided.

F·6

Thesis document left to the last three weeks while the code was “almost done”.

The antidote, and the deliverable hiding inside it

  • coachThe antidote to breadth is a pre-committed cut line, written down before building starts, so that adding anything requires consciously amending it. One Feature Only already produced the candidate: el motor de controles.
  • coachAnd the discipline doubles as a deliverable. Delimitación del alcance is a standard thesis section, and every idea cut becomes trabajo futuro — another standard section. The session's excess ideas have a legitimate academic home instead of being waste.
  • coachArc closed: the original proposal said the project works “siempre que se defina adecuadamente el alcance”. This whole session has been the act of defining it.

The one scope change — and it went the wrong way on purpose

D, autoría sin código, promoted from Should to Must. The student's reason: “it's part of the flow.” If the only way to produce a control definition is the student editing JSON, then the encargado de cumplimiento is not a user of the system, and the platform claim dies with her.

The coach split D so the promotion stays survivable:

D1 · architecture, always MustThe engine consumes declarative control definitions.
D2 · the authoring UI, now MustIf it stalls, seed definitions directly. The engine is unaffected, the demo survives, one screen is lost instead of the architecture. Needs a pre-committed cutoff date.
